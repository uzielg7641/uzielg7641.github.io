## 🔧 Troubleshooting

Most issues are easy to fix. Here are the most common problems and solutions:

### HeadOrbit doesn't detect my AirPods
- Make sure your AirPods are **connected** to your Mac. Go to **System Preferences** → **Bluetooth** and confirm your AirPods show as "Connected."
- Restart HeadOrbit: click the menu bar icon and select **Quit**, then reopen the app.
- Try re-pairing your AirPods: put them back in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then reconnect.

### The blur effect is too strong or too weak
- Open HeadOrbit's menu and adjust the **Blur Strength** slider.
- Turn down **Reaction Speed** if it activates too quickly — set it to 5 seconds for a more relaxed experience.

### HeadOrbit crashes on launch
- Make sure you're on a recent version of macOS (Big Sur 11.0 or later is recommended).
- Download the latest version of HeadOrbit from the [releases page](https://github.com/uzielg7641/HeadOrbit/releases) — older builds may have bugs that were already fixed.

### Head tracking feels inaccurate
- Ensure your AirPods are snugly fitted in your ears.
- Avoid strong magnetic fields or metal objects near your ears — they can interfere with the sensors.
- Calibrate by restarting both your AirPods and HeadOrbit.
